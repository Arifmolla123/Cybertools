How it works

YouTube serves every thumbnail at predictable URLs. This tool reads the video ID from a link and pulls all five standard sizes straight from YouTube's image servers. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no server, no tracking.

Resolutions

  • Max HD — 1280×720 (not always available)
  • SD — 640×480
  • HQ — 480×360 (always available)
  • MQ — 320×180
  • Default — 120×90

Tips

  • Works with watch, youtu.be, shorts, embed, and live links.
  • If download stalls, tap Open and long-press → Save.
  • Max HD missing on some videos — YouTube only generates it for HD sources.

Privacy

No analytics. No cookies. No data leaves your device except image requests to YouTube's servers.
