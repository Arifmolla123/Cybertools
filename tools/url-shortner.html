URL Shortener

Shorten large links, share easily — copy with one click
✨ Your shortened link will appear here.

📘 How to use?

  1. Enter your long link in the box. (https:// Start with)
  2. “Shorten” Click the button.
  3. Your short link will be created below. “Copy link” Clicking the button will copy it to the clipboard.
  4. Send the short link to anyone. When they click on the link, they will be taken to the real link.
  5. All the links you create are stored here (the browser's localStorage). “Delete history” You can delete it by clicking the button.
